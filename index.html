OUR MISSION

RAISING WHOLE CHILDREN
IN FAITH & SKILL.

Uncle Ducky is a Christian micro school teacher rooted in the belief that every child is uniquely created by God, and deserves an education that honors that. We go far beyond textbooks, equipping children with practical, real-world skills alongside a solid Biblical foundation.

Serving families across Richmond, Williamsburg, New Kent County, James City County, York County, Gloucester, Hampton, and Newport News — we bring together the best of homeschool flexibility and structured classroom community.

MEET YOUR TEACHER

Dustin Schmidt aka "Uncle Ducky" is a lifelong educator, coach, mentor, sailor, athlete, and builder with a passion for meeting children where they are and helping them discover their God-given gifts — inside the classroom, out on the athletic field, and exploring nature together.
Make sure to check out his YouTube channel The Prayer Warrior for biblical insights and prayers for any season!

WHAT WE TEACH

SUBJECTS THAT
ACTUALLY MATTER.

Every subject is taught through a Biblical worldview, with an emphasis on doing — not just memorizing.

━━━ TECHNOLOGY TRACK ━━━
🔬
STEM
Science, Technology, Engineering & Math woven together through hands-on experiments and real projects.
💻
WEB DESIGN & CODING
HTML, CSS, JavaScript fundamentals. Students build real websites they can show the world.
🤖
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
Age-appropriate AI literacy — how it works, how to use it wisely, and how to think critically about it.
🖥️
BASIC COMPUTER SKILLS
Typing, file management, productivity tools, internet safety, and responsible digital citizenship.
━━━ OUTDOOR & LIFE SKILLS TRACK ━━━
⛵
SAILING
From knot-tying to reading wind and tide — students learn to skipper on the waters of coastal Virginia.
🎣
FISHING
Rod rigging, bait selection, casting, fish identification, and the patience that comes with it.
🌱
GARDENING & HORTICULTURE
Planting, growing, composting, and caring for the earth God created — from seed to harvest.
🔨
BASIC CARPENTRY & MECHANICS
Measuring, cutting, building, and fixing things. Practical skills every young person should know.
━━━ ARTS & ATHLETICS TRACK ━━━
🎭
ACTING & DRAMA
Stage presence, script reading, public speaking, storytelling, and the confidence that comes from performing.
🥍
LACROSSE
One of America's oldest sports — stick skills, teamwork, field positioning, and Christian sportsmanship.
🏈
FOOTBALL
Fundamentals, routes, blocking, and the discipline that team sports build in young athletes.
🏀
BASKETBALL
Shooting, passing, defense, and how to win and lose with grace — character through competition.
⚾
BASEBALL
Pitching mechanics, batting fundamentals, base running, and the strategy behind America's pastime.
⚽
SOCCER
Footwork, positioning, set plays, and the endurance and teamwork that the world's game demands.
